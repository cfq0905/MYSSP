    What this research does

    Malay emotion datasets are small and uneven. We first create better Malay training text with EAT, then train MEPC to classify emotion across English, Malay, and Chinese.

    EAT

    Translates English GoEmotions into Malay while keeping the emotion tone (GoMS).

    MEPC

    Learns emotion clusters with XLM-RoBERTa using episodic prototypes and contrastive training.

    Labels

    anger, disgust, joy, neutral, sadness, surprise (fear removed — too few examples).

    Key result

    MEPC–EAT: BalAcc 0.5983 · Macro-F1 0.5829 · Weighted-F1 0.6095
